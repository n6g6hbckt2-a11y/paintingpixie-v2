A note from Kat

Why I love Christmas

Christmas is my favourite time of year, and a lot of that is down to my daughter. Every morning in November and December we ask Alexa how many days it is until Christmas. She tells us, then shares a Christmas joke, usually a terrible one, which makes it even better!

Watching my daughter get giddy about advent calendars, the tree going up and Santa’s visit reminds me how magical Christmas feels when you’re little. That’s the feeling I want to bring to your party. A sparkly snowflake or a cheeky Rudolph is a small thing, but seeing a child run off to show their friends never gets old. I can’t wait to be part of your celebrations.

Kat x

Christmas questions

When should I book Christmas face painting?

As early as you can. December weekends go first, especially the Saturdays before Christmas. Send Kat your date now, even if the details aren’t fixed yet.

Can you come on a weekday evening?

Yes. Weekend parties can start from 9am and weekday parties from 5:30pm, so an after-school Christmas party is a good option if your weekend is taken.

How much does a Christmas party cost?

The same as the rest of the year: the Classic Party is from £130 for 2 hours and the Ultimate Sparkle is from £160 for 3 hours with glitter and gems. Grown-up Christmas parties start from £150.

What do I need to provide?

Nothing special. Kat brings all her own equipment, including her table and paints, and sets up and packs away within your booking. Just let her know where you’d like her to set up.

Do you paint grown-ups too?

Yes. Glitter, gems and grown-up designs for Christmas get-togethers and hen dos, from £150. See adult face painting and the glitter bar.

Can you do a Christmas fair or bigger event?

Yes. For Christmas fairs, Kat can run a face painting stall where families pay per face, or come for a set fee, whichever suits your fair. For bigger events, tell Kat roughly how many people and how long it runs, and she will quote for it.

Can you work alongside Santa’s grotto?

Yes. Face painting is a lovely way to keep children happy while they queue for Santa at garden centres, farms and community events. Ask Kat for a quote.

Can you come to an office Christmas party?

Yes. Glitter, gems and grown-up designs for staff parties, or a kids’ corner at family work events. Ask Kat for a quote.

Do you take New Year’s Eve bookings?

Yes. Glitter, gems and sparkly party looks for New Year’s Eve, for grown-ups and children. Send Kat your plans for a quote.

What happens if I need to rearrange?

Let Kat know as soon as you can and she will move your booking to a new date if she is free, with your deposit carried over. If you need to cancel instead: 14 days or more before the party, the deposit is refunded in full; 7 to 13 days before, half is refunded; less than 7 days before, it isn’t refunded but can still be used to rearrange.

Are you insured and DBS checked?

Yes. Kat is fully insured, with £6 million cover, and DBS checked.

Is Kat free on your Christmas date?

Tell Kat your date, town and roughly how many children. She will try to reply within 4 hours.
