Christmas questions

When should I book Christmas face painting?

As early as you can. December weekends are the busiest time of Kat's year after Halloween, and Saturdays usually go first. Send your date and Kat will let you know straight away if she's free.

Do you paint at school and PTA Christmas fairs?

Yes. Kat can run a face painting stall where families pay per face, or come for a set fee. Fast, queue-friendly Christmas designs keep the line moving.

Can you come to an office or staff Christmas party?

Yes. Glitter, gems and glitter tattoos are a hit with grown-ups, and a kids' corner works brilliantly at family Christmas events. Ask for a quote.

Can you work alongside Santa's grotto?

Yes. Face painting is a lovely way to keep children happy while they queue for Santa at garden centres, farms and community events.

What Christmas designs do you paint?

Reindeer, snowmen, elves, Christmas puddings, snowflake queens, candy canes, gingerbread, Christmas trees and lots of sparkly stars, plus the classic tigers and unicorns for anyone who wants them.

Is Kat free on your Christmas date?

Tell Kat your date, town and roughly how many children or guests. She usually replies the same day.
