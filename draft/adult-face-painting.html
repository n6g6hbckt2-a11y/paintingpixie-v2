Professional Adult Face Painting for Every Occasion

Adult face painting has moved well beyond kids' parties — I regularly work with hen parties, festivals, brand activations and corporate events across Sussex, Surrey and Brighton, creating sophisticated designs that suit grown-up celebrations. Whether it's glitter and gems for a hen do, bold animal print for a festival look, or branded designs for a corporate event, adult face painting is one of the most requested services I offer.

As a professional, fully insured face painter, I bring the same quality and reliability to adult events as I do to children's parties — fast, skin-safe designs that keep queues moving and guests smiling.

Adult Face Painting Styles

Hen party glam

Glitter, gems and elegant designs for hen dos across Sussex and Surrey.

Animal print looks

Festival & event designs

Statement face art for festivals, brand activations and public events.

Corporate & brand activations
