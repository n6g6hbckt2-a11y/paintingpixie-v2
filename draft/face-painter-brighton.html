“In Brighton, the grown-ups queue for glitter as fast as the kids queue for tigers.”Kat

Brighton is the place where nobody thinks a grown-up with a glittery face is unusual, and I love that. Some of my most creative bookings have been here, from rainbow designs at big celebrations to full glitter looks at hen dos.

In Brighton I focus on events: hen weekends, weddings, festival and Pride days, brand launches and bigger family celebrations where there's a real crowd to paint. Smaller birthday parties are welcome too, so just get in touch and I'll let you know if I can fit yours in. I've recently been out at Macs Farm near Ditchling too, just a short drive away.

A Kat's-eye guide to a Brighton & Hove party

Where

Hotels and seafront venues, wedding venues across the city and the Downs, festival sites, parks and corporate spaces.

When

Brighton Festival in May, summer on the seafront, Pride in August, then Halloween and Christmas events.

What

Hen-do glitter, festival looks, body art, glitter tattoos and a glitter bar, plus a kids' corner for weddings and family events.

Your questions

Do you do hen parties in Brighton?

Yes, lots. Glitter, gems and grown-up designs start from £150, and I can come to your house, Airbnb, venue or hotel.
