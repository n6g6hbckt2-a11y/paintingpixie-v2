“Horsham sits right on the border, so Surrey is on my doorstep.”Kat

Surrey is right on my doorstep. Horsham sits on the border, so towns like Dorking, Reigate and Guildford are an easy drive for parties, weddings and events.

I recently painted at the Eats & Beats Festival at New House Farm, right on the Surrey/Sussex border, and at the last Guildford Festival of the Arts too. Whether it's a garden party or a large-scale event, I cover towns across Surrey and I'm happy to travel further for the right booking.

Surrey questions

Which parts of Surrey do you cover?

Mainly south and central Surrey: Guildford, Reigate, Redhill, Dorking, Cranleigh, Leatherhead and Horley.

Do you charge for travel?

For longer trips I may add a small travel charge, always agreed up front.

Can you paint at a Surrey wedding?

Yes. Face painting and glitter are lovely for guests of all ages.

Can you bring more than one artist?

Yes, for big events I can bring extra artists.
