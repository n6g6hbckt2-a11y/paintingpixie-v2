How Booking Works

Getting a date confirmed is simple. Message me with your event date, location and rough headcount — I'll usually reply the same day with availability and a firm quote. Weekend dates in Horsham, Crawley, Guildford and across Sussex and Surrey book up several weeks ahead, especially over the summer and around Christmas, so it's worth reaching out early if you have a fixed date in mind. Once you're happy to go ahead, a small deposit secures your booking.

Where I Cover

Based in Horsham, I regularly travel across West Sussex and Surrey — including Crawley, Guildford, Reigate, Haywards Heath, Dorking, Worthing, Burgess Hill, Brighton, Lewes, East Grinstead, and villages like Billingshurst, Henfield, Steyning and Cranleigh. Not sure if your event falls within range? Just ask — I'm happy to travel further for larger bookings.
