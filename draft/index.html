Good to know

Questions parents ask.

Where do you travel to?

Kat is based in Horsham and paints across West Sussex, East Sussex, Brighton and Surrey, including Crawley, Haywards Heath, Worthing, Guildford, Reigate and Dorking. Bigger events further afield are welcome too.

How long should I book Kat for?

The Classic Party is 2 hours of face painting and the Ultimate Sparkle is 3 hours. Tell Kat roughly how many guests you are expecting and she will suggest the right length of booking, or bring an extra artist for big events.

Are the paints safe for children's skin?

Yes. Kat uses professional, cosmetic-grade face paints made for skin, and bio-glitter. If a child has sensitive skin, a small patch test on the arm is a good idea.

Do you paint adults and hen parties?

Yes. Hen parties and adult celebrations start from £150, with glitter, gems and grown-up designs. Kat also runs a chunky glitter and jewel bar for festivals and weddings.

How far ahead should I book?

Weekend dates often book several weeks in advance, and Halloween and Christmas go first. A small deposit secures your date.

Are you insured and DBS checked?

Yes. Kat is fully insured and DBS checked, and can provide insurance details and a risk assessment for venues and events.
