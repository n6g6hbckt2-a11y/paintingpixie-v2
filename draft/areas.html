Professional face painting for adults and children at parties, weddings and events across Sussex and Surrey.

The Painting Pixie proudly brings high-quality face painting, festival glitter, and party entertainment to events across Sussex and Surrey. We regularly service Horsham, Crawley, Haywards Heath, Burgess Hill, Dorking, Reigate, Chichester, Arundel, Worthing, Brighton, Lewes, Tunbridge Wells, Croydon, Epsom, Farnham, East Grinstead, Redhill, Guildford, and the surrounding local areas. Click your area below for local details, or get in touch if you don't see your town listed — I'm happy to travel across Sussex and Surrey and have in the past been fortunate enough to work in Portsmouth, Southampton, Eastbourne, Hastings, Maidstone and Sevenoaks
