Professional Face Painting for Children's Parties

Children's birthday parties are where it all started for me — I love bringing colour, fun and a bit of magic to kids' celebrations across Sussex, Surrey and Brighton. From unicorns and princesses to dragons and superheroes, I keep queues moving with fast, friendly designs that keep the party going. If you're looking for a face painter near me for your child's birthday, you've found Sussex and Surrey's favourite children's entertainer — I bring the magic straight to your garden, hall or venue.

As a professional, fully insured and DBS-checked face painter, I use premium skin-safe paints so parents can relax while the kids have a magical time.
