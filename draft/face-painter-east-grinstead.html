A note from Kat

I painted faces at an event in East Grinstead recently and came home completely charmed. The high street is a picture, with that long run of old timber-framed buildings, and the families were so friendly.

It's a town with a lot of history, from the Bluebell Railway steaming in at the station to Ashdown Forest just down the road, and I love that a party here can feel like part of a story.

If you're planning something in East Grinstead or the villages around it, I'd love to come back.

Kat x

“Old timber-framed streets, steam trains and the forest on the doorstep. East Grinstead has a bit of magic built in.”

Party spots around East Grinstead

  1. East CourtLovely grounds and a classic spot for town events.
  2. Mount NoddyThe recreation ground for summer fun days and picnics.
  3. Chequer MeadThe arts centre, right in town.
  4. Weir Wood ReservoirA beautiful backdrop on the edge of town.
  5. Ashdown ForestHome of the Hundred Acre Wood, a short drive away.
  6. Village hallsForest Row, Felbridge and Sharpthorne all have great ones.

East Grinstead questions

Do you travel to East Grinstead from Horsham?

Yes, it's an easy drive and I'm happy to come over for parties, fairs and events.

Do you cover Forest Row and Lingfield?

Yes, along with Felbridge, Dormansland and the villages around Ashdown Forest.

Can you paint at a school fair?

Yes. I can work as a paid stall or for a set fee, and I keep designs quick so the queue keeps moving.

Is the paint safe for sensitive skin?

Yes. I use professional, cosmetic-grade face paints made for skin.
