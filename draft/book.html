Travel

Is there a travel charge?

No charge within 15 miles of Kat's home in Horsham. Further away, travel is 55p a mile, there and back, for the miles beyond 15. Check your postcode:

An estimate by distance; Kat confirms the exact mileage when she confirms your booking. Travel is added to the balance, not the deposit.

How it works

From booking to party day

  1. Choose
    Pick the 2-hour or 3-hour party.
  2. Pick a date
    Only dates and times Kat is free are shown.
  3. Pay the deposit
    One third of the price, by card.
  4. Party day
    Kat arrives, sets up and paints. The balance and any travel are paid on the day.

Booking terms

Deposits and changes

  • The deposit is one third of the party price (a maximum of £60).
  • The balance, plus any travel, is paid on the day.
  • Cancel 14 days or more before the party: full refund of the deposit.
  • Cancel 7 to 13 days before: 50% of the deposit is refunded.
  • Less than 7 days before: the deposit isn't refunded, but it can be used to rearrange to a new date.
