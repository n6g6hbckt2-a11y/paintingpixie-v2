Face Painting for Horsham Parties & Events

Horsham is my home — I live right here in town, and there's nowhere I love painting faces more. I recently painted at the Eats & Beats Festival at New House Farm on the outskirts of town — a brilliant day meeting so many local families. Whether it's a birthday party in Denne Park or Hills Farm, a festival, a school fete, a wedding or a community event, there's something special about bringing a bit of magic to celebrations in my own home town.

From an intimate garden party to a bigger celebration at a Horsham venue, you'll get a fully insured, DBS-checked professional with fast, high-quality designs that keep the queue moving and the children smiling.
