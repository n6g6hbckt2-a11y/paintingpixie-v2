A Dedicated Glitter Bar for Your Event

As a professional glitter artist, I bring a dedicated Chunky Glitter & Jewel Bar to parties, festivals, hen dos and weddings across Sussex, Surrey and Brighton. It's the perfect stand-alone activity or a glamorous add-on to face painting — fast-moving, endlessly Instagrammable, and loved by all ages. From festival glitter in Sussex to hen party glitter across Surrey and Brighton, my Chunky Glitter & Jewel Bar brings professional, skin-safe sparkle to any celebration.

Using premium bio-glitter, face gems and jewels, I create bold chunky glitter looks and delicate sparkle finishes side by side, so there's something for everyone in the queue — from little ones after a rainbow cheek swipe to adults wanting a full glitter moment.
