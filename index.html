Where the ideas come from

None of the three copies a single website. Each one takes a proven approach from well-designed small UK businesses and applies it to Kat's photos, prices and reviews. These are the clearest real examples of each approach.

A · Sage & Gold: calm, premium and plenty of white space, the way upmarket independent florists and wedding suppliers present themselves.
Example: Grace & Thorn, an independent Hackney florist that Vogue and Time Out name among the UK's best.
B · Watercolour: bright, friendly and built around "what's the occasion?", like the best-known children's party companies.
Examples: Sharky & George and Sublime Science, both picked by The Good Web Guide as UK kids' party companies to know.
C · Photo-led: big photos on a dark background so the work sells itself, the approach used by top wedding and portrait photographers.
Examples: Squaremuse's collection of dark, photo-led sites.
All three share the same booking features, chosen from conversion research rather than looks: prices shown up front, reviews beside every button, a one-tap WhatsApp message and a booking bar fixed to the bottom of phone screens.
