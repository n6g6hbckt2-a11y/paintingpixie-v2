D is based on Taylor Entertainment (Orange County & LA). A small, boutique face-painting business with 500+ five-star Yelp reviews. A price calculator at the top of their homepage gives real prices with "no form, no waiting on a quote", and the headline leads with their reviews.
E is based on Bazinga Parties (New York), which has prices by number of children, a "how it happens" section, Google reviews and a 10-question FAQ. Also Wowzers (40+ US cities), whose town pages list the surrounding neighbourhoods and answer practical questions. Google ranks pages like these well for "face painter + town".
F is based on Yombu (US). It asks for city, date and number of children first, then shows options, prices and the cost per child, with reassurance such as free cancellation and payment held until after the party.

Where A, B and C come from

None of the three copies a single website. They take the best ideas from the strongest small businesses close to Kat's model and apply them to her photos, prices and reviews.

A · Sage & Gold: premium polish, so a children's business can look upmarket and charge accordingly.
Les Enfants: luxury London children's party planner. Real event photos and a named founder.
Kid City Parties: sells the experience, with venue logos for trust and a clear order: promise, options, proof, founder, enquire.
B · Watercolour: the friendly, family-party alternative. It's here for comparison. The research above points towards a more premium look than this.
C · Photo-led: the work is the main picture, with a dark premium look.
Luminosity Glitter: closest to Kat's service (glitter, face art, corporate). Black premium styling and a "worked alongside" logo strip.
Painted Pets: solo artist, Rocketspark design award winner July 2026. Founder story near the top, "how it works", and the work as the hero.
All three: how the page sells, rather than how it looks.
Packaway Parties: prices shown early (close to Kat's), lots of reviews with places and dates, and an FAQ that answers worries before anyone asks.
Absolute Booth: named products with their own pages, county pages, and the review count up front.
