Professional Face Painting Across Surrey

I'm Kat, and I regularly travel across Surrey for families, schools and event organisers — from birthday parties in Guildford and Reigate to weddings and hen dos in Dorking and beyond. Wherever you're celebrating in Surrey, I bring a fully insured, professional face painting service with fast, high-quality designs that keep queues moving and guests smiling. I recently painted at the Eats & Beats Festival at New House Farm, right on the Surrey/Sussex border near Horsham and Crawley, and at the last Guildford Festival of the Arts too!

Whether you need a children's entertainer for a garden party or professional face painters for a larger event, I cover towns across Surrey and I'm happy to travel further for the right booking.
