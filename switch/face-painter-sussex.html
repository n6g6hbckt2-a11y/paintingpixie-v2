“From the Weald to the coast, Sussex is where I paint most.”Kat

I'm Kat, I live in Horsham, and Sussex is where I paint most. From Horsham, Crawley and Haywards Heath in West Sussex to Brighton, Lewes and the East Sussex coast, I paint at children's parties, hen dos, festivals and events right across the county.

I recently painted at the Eats & Beats Festival at New House Farm near Horsham, and I was out at Macs Farm near Ditchling too. Whether it's a birthday party in the garden, adult face painting for a hen do, or a glitter bar at a festival, I cover towns across West and East Sussex and I'm happy to travel further for the right booking.

Sussex questions

Which parts of Sussex do you cover?

All of West Sussex, Brighton & Hove and much of East Sussex, from Horsham and Crawley to Worthing, Brighton and Lewes.

Do you charge for travel?

Not within 15 miles of my home in Horsham. Further away, travel is 55p a mile for the extra miles, there and back, and I always confirm it before you book.

Do you paint adults and hen parties in Sussex?

Yes. Hen dos and adult parties start from £150, with glitter, gems and grown-up designs, including in Brighton.

Do you do festivals and events in Sussex?

Yes. Festivals, school fairs and corporate events are all welcome: ask Kat for a quote.
