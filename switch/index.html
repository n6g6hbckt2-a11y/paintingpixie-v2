Good to know

Questions parents ask

Where do you travel to?

Kat is based in Horsham and paints across West Sussex, East Sussex, Brighton and Surrey, including Crawley, Haywards Heath, Worthing, Guildford, Reigate and Dorking. Bigger events further afield are welcome too. See every area on the map.

How much does it cost?

Children’s parties start at £130 for 2 hours (Classic Party) or £160 for 3 hours with glitter and gems (Ultimate Sparkle). Hen parties and adult celebrations start at £150. Weddings, festivals and corporate events are quoted individually.

How long should I book Kat for?

Tell Kat roughly how many guests you are expecting and she will suggest the right length: the Classic Party is 2 hours and the Ultimate Sparkle is 3 hours.

Are the paints safe for children’s skin?

Yes. Kat uses professional, skin-safe face paints and bio-glitter. If a child has sensitive skin, a small patch test on the arm is a good idea.

Do you paint adults and hen parties?

Yes. Hen parties and adult celebrations start from £150, with glitter, gems and grown-up designs. See adult face painting and the glitter bar.

How far ahead should I book?

Weekend dates often book several weeks in advance, and Halloween and Christmas go first. A deposit secures your date.

Are you insured and DBS checked?

Yes. Kat is fully insured and DBS checked.
