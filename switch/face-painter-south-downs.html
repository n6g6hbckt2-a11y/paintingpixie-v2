Face Painting in the South Downs

Midhurst and Petworth are a little further out on the South Downs, but I love the drive out that way for birthday parties, school fetes, weddings and community events. It's beautiful countryside, and I'm always happy to travel for a good celebration.

Whether you're planning a small garden party or a larger event, I bring a fully insured, DBS-checked, professional face painting service with fast, high-quality designs that keep queues moving and children smiling.

Face Painting Styles & Services

Children's party face painting

Classic designs, superheroes, animals and princesses for birthday parties and family events.

Adult face & body art

Hen parties, festivals, brand activations and corporate events.

Animal print designs

Glitter art

Glitter freckles, glitter tattoos and sparkle accents for a bit of extra magic.
