Corporate Event Questions

How much does corporate face painting cost?

Every corporate booking is quoted individually, based on your date, location, guest numbers and how long you need Kat. The glitter and jewel bar starts from £140 for 2 hours. Send your details and Kat will come back with a clear quote.

How many guests can you paint?

It depends on the designs people choose: quick glitter and cheek designs keep a busy queue moving, while full faces take longer. Tell Kat your guest numbers and she'll suggest how many hours you need so nobody misses out.

Can you match our brand colours or theme?

Yes. Kat can paint designs in your brand colours or to match an event theme, from brand-colour swirls and flowers to festive designs for a Christmas party.

Do you cover London?

Yes. Kat works across Sussex and Surrey and regularly travels into central London for events. London bookings are quoted individually.

Do you bring UV lights for neon face painting?

The neon paint glows under UV light, which the venue or your DJ usually provides. If your venue doesn't have UV lighting, Kat can still paint bright neon designs, and your venue or DJ may be able to add UV lights.

Are you insured and DBS checked?

Yes. Kat is fully insured with £6 million public liability cover and DBS checked, and can send her insurance details for your venue or events team.

What do you need on the day?

A table and two chairs in a well-lit spot, ideally near where your guests gather. Kat brings everything else and sets up and packs away.
