Grown-Up Glitter and Face Art

Hen dos, big birthdays, festivals and work parties: I paint grown-up looks that are made for photos. Think chunky bio-glitter, face gems, glitter roots, neon that glows under UV light and bold animal print, matched to your theme or outfits.

I come to you, at home, in a hotel or at your venue, across Sussex, Surrey and Brighton, and further afield for bigger events. I'm fully insured and DBS checked, I bring everything, and I keep the queue moving so everyone is ready before the night out.

Hen & Adult Party Questions

How much does hen party face painting cost?

Hen parties and adult celebrations start from £150. Kat confirms the exact price for your date, and a small deposit secures your booking.

Where can you paint?

At your house, an Airbnb, a hotel or a venue anywhere in Sussex, Surrey and Brighton, and further afield for bigger events.

How many people can you paint?

It depends on the designs your group chooses. Tell Kat how many are coming and she will suggest 2 or 3 hours so everyone is painted.

Is the glitter safe and eco-friendly?

Yes. Kat uses professional, skin-safe paints and biodegradable bio-glitter, plus cosmetic face gems.

How do I get it off?

Wipe away loose glitter first, then use warm water and a gentle face wash or make-up remover. Gems peel off easily.

Do you do corporate and festival events?

Yes. Staff parties, product launches, brand activations and festivals, with bespoke or brand-colour designs. Ask Kat for a quote.

Are you insured and DBS checked?

Yes. Kat is fully insured, with £6 million cover, and DBS checked.
