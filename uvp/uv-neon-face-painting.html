UV Neon & Glitter Face Painting for Glow Parties & Evening Events

Neon face paint and glitter that glows under UV light, for glow parties, discos, 18ths, hen nights and evening events across Sussex, Surrey and Brighton.

Face Paint That Glows

UV neon face painting uses bright fluorescent paints that light up under ultraviolet (blacklight) lighting. In normal light the designs are bold and colourful; when the UV lights go on, they glow. I paint neon flowers, swirls, rainbow stripes, stars and glow-in-the-dark skulls, and add chunky glitter and gems for extra sparkle.

It's a brilliant fit for anything that happens after dark: glow parties, children's and teen discos, 18th and 21st birthdays, hen nights, evening weddings, festivals and corporate evening events across Sussex, Surrey and Brighton.

Planning a Glow Party?

UV lighting: the blacklight comes from your venue or party host. Many UV discos and party DJs bring it, or it can be hired. I'll paint in good light and the designs come alive when the UV goes on.

Mix and match: UV neon can be added to any face painting or glitter booking, or booked on its own for your event.

Professional and safe: fully insured, DBS checked, and 5.0 rated.

Questions

Do you bring UV lights?

No. The UV (blacklight) lighting comes from the venue or host. Many UV discos and party DJs bring it, or it can be hired. The designs still look bright and colourful in normal light.

What events is UV neon face painting good for?

Glow parties, children's and teen discos, 18th and 21st birthdays, hen nights, evening weddings, festivals after dark and corporate evening events.

Can I mix UV neon with glitter?

Yes. Neon paint and chunky glitter work beautifully together, and many guests choose both.
